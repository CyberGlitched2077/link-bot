How to Apply

MESMER offers MSME loans with lower interest rate and flexible collateral requirements.

Eligibility

An applicant must fulfill the following criteria.
  • Formal Enterprise

    Enterprises must have valid business license, TIN certificate, ID number

  • Enterprises in operation

    Enterprises that have started and are in operation.

  • In All regions of Ethiopia

    In All regions of Ethiopia (Afar, Amhara, Benishangul-Gumuz, Central Ethiopia, Dire Dawa, Gambella, Harari, Oromia, Sidama, Somali, South Ethiopia, South West Ethiopia and Tigray )

Form Instructions

Applicants must complete the following fields and attach supporting documents.
  • Personal Information

    Full name in English, age, sex, marital status, disability status, has smartphone

  • Residential Address

    Region, subcity, woreda, zone or area

  • Business Address

    Region, subcity, woreda, zone or area

  • Monthly Income Status

    Amount of money you earn in a month from your salary income.

  • Employment Status

    Number of employees by age group and by gender

  • Create Account

    Before submitting an application, users are required to create an account on our platform to access the application form and ensure that their information is securely stored for future reference.

  • Supporting Documents

    Residence (Kebele) ID, Bank book, Business License, TIN number, Number of employees, Evidence of the business presence in the area (one photo)

  • Employment

    Number of employees by age group and by gender

Application Process

Step 1:

Register or Sign up by providing your full name, phone number and memorable password (must be 8 characters). Agree to the Terms and Conditions

Step 2:

Sign in using the authentication code sent to your phone eg.(XB3445). Please input the code in the space provided.

Step 3:

Complete the application form for Credit if you have a formal enterprise.

Step 4:

Applicants are screened based on the eligibility and selection criteria

Step 5:

Applicants that pass the first screening attend business development training

Step 6:

Enterprises that complete the business development training are transferred to one of the four banks for assessment

Step 7:

Enterprises are notified by SMS which bank their case has been transferred to. They then start

Step 8:

Provide all documents required by the bank and receive credit from the bank

Step 9:

Make regular repayments of the credit as per the agreed repayment schedule

Objective

Targets to reach 72,200 MSMEs, startups and informal enterprises with access to credit, grant, Business development and psychosocial support services, and support 410,800 works within these enterprises.

Duration

October 2022 - September 2027

Geography

In All regions of Ethiopia (Afar, Amhara, Benishangul-Gumuz, Central Ethiopia, Dire Dawa, Gambella, Harari, Oromia, Sidama, Somali, South Ethiopia, South West Ethiopia and Tigray )

MESMER Program

MESMER is a program that aims to help enterprises by providing access to finance, business development, and psychosocial services. The goal is to help these businesses recover from shocks, build resilience and grow to create dignified and fulfilling work for the youth.
